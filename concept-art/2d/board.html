Gravel Riot
concept board

Fifty frames for an all-terrain festival racer in the spirit of MotorStorm: mixed-class races where bikes, buggies, rally cars, mud pluggers, trucks and big rigs share the same track, across terrain that breaks down as the race goes on. Click any frame to view it large.

All frames are original procedural illustrations drawn for this board; "Gravel Riot" is a working title. Vehicle classes follow a mixed-field rule: lighter classes are faster and more agile on firm ground, heavier classes plough through mud and shove rivals aside.
