Gravel Riot
gameplay snapshots

Twenty in-engine style captures for a MotorStorm-inspired racer. Buggies, rally cars, trophy trucks, mud pluggers, monster trucks and big rigs share one track across canyons, mud valleys, volcanoes, ice fields, jungle, coast and a ruined city. Every frame is a lit 3D scene rendered with path tracing, motion blur and a race HUD. Click any frame to view it full size.

Scenes, vehicles and props are modelled procedurally and rendered in Blender Cycles. They show lighting, mood, camera and vehicle-class direction; production art would add hand-modelled cars, drivers and textured terrain. "Gravel Riot" is a working title.
