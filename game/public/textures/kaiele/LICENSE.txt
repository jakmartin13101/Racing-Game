Kai'ele ground, road and sky textures. All sources are CC0 1.0 (public domain); shrunk to 1K and packed
(<key>_ar.webp: albedo + roughness in alpha; <key>_nao.jpg: normal XY + ambient occlusion).

sand     ambientCG Ground093C        https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground093C
grass    ambientCG Grass004          https://ambientcg.com/a/Grass004
litter   ambientCG Ground023         https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground023
dirt     ambientCG Ground053         https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground053
rock     ambientCG Rock035 (albedo brightened x4 in linear light)  https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock035
coral    ambientCG Gravel023         https://ambientcg.com/a/Gravel023
mud      ambientCG Ground051         https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground051
planks   Poly Haven weathered_planks https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_planks
sky      Poly Haven kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky (Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest), 4K HDR encoded to an 8-bit
         dome texture: RGB = (L / lmax) ^ (1/2.2), from the zenith to 8 degrees below the horizon (see sky.json)
         https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
